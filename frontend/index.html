Operational Rules

  • Always save stego images as PNG: Lossy compression formats (such as JPEG) alter high-frequency pixel values and destroy embedded carrier maps.
  • Maintain geometric consistency: Resizing, cropping, or rotating stego images will shift coordinate grids and cause Reed–Solomon decoding to fail.
  • Unauthenticated rejection: Any password mismatch or pixel tampering results in an immediate authentication error; no invalid plaintext is ever leaked.
